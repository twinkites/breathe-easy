About

Tree locations from OpenStreetMap via Overpass API. Pollen forecasts & air quality from Open-Meteo (free, no key required).

🔒 Your data stays with you. All preferences are stored locally in your browser. Your location and search queries are sent directly to the data providers (OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo, Nominatim) and are never routed through or stored on any Breathe Easy server. We do not track your requests.

⚠️ Not medical advice. Pollen data is modeled and may not reflect your exact conditions. Air quality readings may be delayed or inaccurate. Do not rely on this app for medical decisions. Consult a healthcare provider regarding allergy management.
