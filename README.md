# Breathe Easy

Know what's in the air before you head outside.

Breathe Easy shows you hyper-local pollen sources and air quality for wherever you are — trees, grasses, and weeds right in your neighborhood.

No account, no ads, no tracking.

---

## What it shows you

**Today's pollen forecast** — Live readings for birch, alder, olive, grass, ragweed, and mugwort pollen, pulled fresh from Open-Meteo's forecast model.

**Best time to go outside** — An hourly chart of pollen levels throughout the day, with a recommendation for the lowest-pollen window.

**5-day forecast** — See whether pollen is trending up or down over the coming week.

**Air quality** — Current PM2.5, PM10, ozone, and NO₂ readings for your location.

**Rain conditions** — If rain is falling or expected soon, Breathe Easy lets you know — rain washes pollen out of the air and is often a good time to be outside.

**Wind direction** — Shows which way pollen is drifting on the map, so you can see where it's blowing from and heading to.

**Nearby pollen sources on the map** — Individual trees tagged in OpenStreetMap, colored by species and pollen risk. Grass and meadow areas are shown as shaded polygons.

**Pollen heatmap** — A color overlay (green → red) that helps you find lower-pollen pockets near you.

---

## How to use it

1. **Enter your location** — Type a ZIP code, address, or city name into the search box and press Go. Or tap **Try to Locate Me** to use your device's GPS.

2. **Read the panel** — The left panel shows today's pollen levels, hourly chart, forecast, and air quality. Scroll down for rain and wind info.

3. **Explore the map** — Zoom and pan to see tree markers and grass areas. Tap any marker for details about that specific tree — species, pollen risk, and its pollination calendar.

4. **Set your allergen profile** — Under **My Allergens**, tap the types you're sensitive to. Pollen cards for your allergens will be highlighted, others dimmed, and the heatmap will filter to only show your relevant sources.

5. **Toggle map layers** — Use the **Show on Map** toggles to turn trees, grasses, wind arrows, and the heatmap on or off.

6. **Adjust the search radius** — The radius slider in the top bar controls how far out the app looks for trees (500 m to 5 km).

## Understanding the risk levels

| Color    | Level      | What it means                             |
| -------- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| 🟢 Green  | Low        | Unlikely to cause symptoms                |
| 🟡 Yellow | Moderate   | Mild symptoms possible                    |
| 🟠 Orange | High       | Likely to cause symptoms                  |
| 🔴 Red    | Very High  | Severe symptoms likely                    |
| 🟣 Purple | Off-season | Tree exists but not currently pollinating |

## Your privacy

Everything stays in your browser. Your location and searches go directly to OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo, and Nominatim — they never touch any Breathe Easy server, and nothing is stored or tracked on our end. Your allergen profile and map preferences are saved locally on your device only.

---

## Install on your phone

Breathe Easy works as an app on your home screen — no app store required.

- **iPhone/iPad:** Open in Safari → tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen"
- **Android:** Open in Chrome → tap the three-dot menu → "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"

Once installed it works offline, showing your last loaded data when you don't have a connection.

---

## A note on accuracy

Pollen data comes from a forecast model, not a physical sensor in your neighborhood. Real conditions vary based on local microclimates, which specific trees are blooming near you, and weather patterns. Tree locations come from volunteer-contributed OpenStreetMap data and may not include every tree.

**This app is not medical advice.** Do not use it to make decisions about medication or medical treatment. Talk to your doctor or allergist about managing your allergies.
